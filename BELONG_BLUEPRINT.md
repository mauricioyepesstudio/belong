# BELONG

## The Life Operating System

BELONG is not a productivity application.

BELONG is an operating system designed to help every person discover purpose, build meaningful projects, collaborate with the right people, create measurable impact, and continuously evolve throughout life.

Everything inside BELONG exists to help people become who they are capable of becoming.

---

# Vision

Create the world's operating system for human growth.

BELONG exists to help billions of people discover who they are, why they matter, what they should build, and how they can create meaningful impact throughout their lives.

---

# Mission

Increase human potential.

One person.

One project.

One community.

One mission at a time.

# Core Architecture

BELONG is built as a collection of independent engines.

The User Interface is only a visualization layer.

Business logic never lives inside UI components.

Every engine owns its own domain.

Every engine exposes adapters.

Every engine can evolve independently.

Every engine should be testable in isolation.
