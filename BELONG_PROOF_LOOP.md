# BELONG Proof Loop

## Product category
BELONG is the Human Action Network.

Other networks primarily organize people around content. BELONG organizes people around purpose, proof, participation, and measurable action.

## Core cultural behavior

**PROVE IT -> SHOW UP -> BUILD -> IMPACT -> BELONG**

The product does not tell people what to believe. It gives testable claims, public commitments, capabilities, and proposed solutions a path to face evidence and real-world action.

## Brand principle

> Aura is what people think you are.
> Proof is what you've actually done.

Aura is a launch/growth metaphor, not a permanent product metric. BELONG must never implement an Aura score, Truth score, ideological score, or universal human score.

## Core objects

### Proof
A structured object around something that can meaningfully face evidence or execution. Proof may begin from a factual claim, causal claim, prediction, public commitment, capability claim, proposed solution, or measurable goal.

### Challenge
An invitation to demonstrate, test, counter, improve, or execute a Proof. A Challenge targets the proposition or proposed outcome, never the dignity or identity of a person.

### Approach
A concrete method proposed to address a Challenge. Multiple approaches may coexist. BELONG should make disagreement productive rather than forcing binary ideological winners.

### Action
A real participation opportunity backed by existing BELONG domains such as Projects, Missions, Events, Communities, Opportunities, Resources, and Show Up actions.

### Evidence
Traceable material attached to a Proof, Approach, Action, or Outcome. Evidence must retain provenance and uncertainty.

### Outcome
The result of applying the predeclared success criteria. Supported, partially supported, mixed, not supported, and inconclusive are valid outcomes. Changing one's position after evidence is a positive behavior, not a failure state.

### Impact Receipt
A shareable record of what happened, who participated when privacy permits, what was verified, and what remains uncertain.

## The end-to-end loop

1. CREATE — A person publishes an idea, claim, commitment, capability, need, or measurable ambition.
2. PROVE IT — BELONG offers a structured path when the object is meaningfully testable.
3. DEFINE — State success criteria, deadline when relevant, evidence requirements, and what result could change the claim.
4. CHALLENGE — Invite other people or organizations to support, counter, improve, test, or execute it.
5. APPROACH — Participants propose one or more ways to produce the desired outcome.
6. SHOW UP — BELONG routes relevant people, skills, organizations, resources, and communities toward concrete roles.
7. BUILD — Existing Projects/Missions/Events/Communities coordinate execution.
8. EVIDENCE — Participants attach traceable evidence and progress updates.
9. OUTCOME — Resolve against the criteria without declaring a person or ideology universally right or wrong.
10. IMPACT — Generate an Impact Receipt and update the participant's Proof Portfolio.
11. SHARE — Export the result to the wider internet as a distribution loop back into BELONG.

## Product verbs

- BELIEVE — identity/alignment signal; not evidence.
- SHOW ME — request demonstration/evidence.
- PROVE IT — open or enter a structured Proof.
- CHALLENGE — propose a competing or stronger test/approach.
- SHOW UP — commit to meaningful participation.
- BUILD — execute with others.
- VERIFY — confirm evidence through an allowed provenance path.
- SHARE PROOF — distribute the resulting receipt.

## Non-negotiable safety and epistemic rules

1. Challenge the claim, test the solution, never dehumanize the person.
2. BELONG does not assign universal Truth Scores or human-worth scores.
3. Identity, dignity, protected traits, personal existence, and subjective lived experience are not Challenge targets.
4. Normative beliefs must not be mislabeled as empirically proven facts.
5. Success criteria should be declared before results whenever practical.
6. Evidence keeps provenance and can be disputed, incomplete, retracted, or superseded.
7. An inconclusive result is a legitimate outcome.
8. Position updates after evidence should be visible as intellectual integrity, not humiliation.
9. Money is only one contribution type. Time, skill, expertise, reach, logistics, relationships, experience, and institutional capacity also count.
10. BELONG optimizes action and constructive participation, not outrage.

## Proof Arena

Arena is the entertainment surface for active Proofs and Challenges. It should feel alive enough to compete with passive social entertainment while remaining grounded in execution.

A live Arena can show:
- question/claim/goal;
- competing or complementary approaches;
- success criteria;
- deadline/progress;
- participants and organizations;
- verified needs;
- live updates/media;
- evidence timeline;
- Watch, Show Up, Challenge, Share actions;
- final Outcome and Impact Receipt.

Arena is not limited to charity. Initial categories can include Community, Creators, Entrepreneurship, Science, Technology, Education, Environment, Culture, Health, Personal Growth, and carefully structured Civic/Policy experiments.

## Proof Passport

The existing BELONG profile/Story Deck should progressively become a Proof Passport:

- Who I am
- My world
- What I care about
- What I'm building
- What I can bring
- What I'm looking for
- What I've shown up for
- What I've proven
- Commitments completed
- Projects delivered
- Bridges built
- Impact Receipts

The product remains aspirational: beauty, art, wealth, travel, achievement, humor, relationships, lifestyle, profession, and ambition remain welcome. BELONG adds depth: what happened because this person showed up?

## Bridge behavior

BELONG should not only optimize similarity. Discovery should eventually support **Productive Difference**: people may disagree strongly while sharing a concrete goal.

A successful cross-view collaboration can create a contextual **Bridge Built** achievement. It must never imply that either participant changed political identity or that BELONG adjudicated their ideology.

## Signal Engine direction

The AI/recommendation layer should answer: **Where can this human matter?**

Future ranking signals may include affinity, declared intent, relevance, trust/provenance, actionability, approximate privacy-safe proximity, impact potential, novelty, and bridge potential.

The user-facing reason must remain legible: e.g. "This project needs your design skill" or "You both care about local education despite different viewpoints."

## North-star product metric

Primary candidate: **Meaningful Actions Completed (MAC)**.

Supporting measures:
- people who Showed Up;
- Proofs resolved;
- commitments completed;
- projects/missions completed;
- Impact Receipts generated;
- cross-group collaborations / Bridges Built;
- external Proof shares that produce new participants.

Watch time, likes, followers, and posting volume remain diagnostic metrics, not the purpose of the system.

## V1 vertical slice

Do not build the entire vision at once. V1 must prove one complete loop using existing engines wherever possible:

1. Proof creation entry point.
2. Structured claim/goal and success criteria.
3. PROVE IT / SHOW ME interaction.
4. Challenge and Approach creation.
5. SHOW UP conversion into an existing Project or Mission.
6. Evidence timeline.
7. Outcome resolution.
8. Impact Receipt generation.
9. Proof displayed on Profile/Story Deck.
10. Shareable deep link.

## V1 demo scenarios

Build only enough seeded/demo presentation to explain the category, while keeping production paths on real BELONG data:

1. Creator mobilization — Can five creators mobilize 10,000 meals in 48 hours?
2. Entrepreneurship — Can 20 founders create 100 jobs in Miami in 30 days?
3. Bridge — Can people who disagree politically complete one community project together?

## Definition of success

A new user seeing the deployed product should understand within roughly one minute:

1. BELONG is not another feed-only social network.
2. PROVE IT means turning a testable statement, commitment, capability, or goal into something evidence/action can resolve.
3. SHOW UP means they can contribute something concrete.
4. Their contribution can become durable, shareable Proof.
5. People who disagree can compete or collaborate without becoming enemies.

Every future core feature should answer one question: **Does this help turn attention into meaningful action?**
