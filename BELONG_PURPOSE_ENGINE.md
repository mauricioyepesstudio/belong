# BELONG Purpose Engine

## Mission

The Purpose Engine helps every person discover why they exist,
what impact they want to create,
and what direction their life should follow.

Purpose is not a goal.

Purpose generates goals.

---

# Engine Objective

Transform uncertainty into clarity.

The engine continuously helps users answer:

> Why do I exist?

---

# Inputs

The engine collects:

- Passions
- Talents
- Skills
- Personal values
- Experiences
- Problems the user wants to solve
- Causes they care about
- Dreams
- Life vision
- Legacy

---

# Outputs

The engine generates:

- Purpose Statement
- Purpose Score
- Alignment Score
- Purpose Evolution
- Confidence Level

---

# Purpose Statement

Example:

"I help young entrepreneurs build technology that improves education."

Another example:

"I create beautiful products that improve people's daily lives."

Purpose is always:

WHO

+

DOES WHAT

+

FOR WHOM

+

WHY

---

# Core Questions

Who do you want to help?

What problems make you angry?

What would you do even if nobody paid you?

What are your greatest strengths?

What impact do you want to leave?

What type of person do you want to become?

---

# AI Responsibilities

The AI never invents purpose.

It discovers purpose.

It asks questions.

It detects patterns.

It proposes hypotheses.

The user always decides.

---

# Purpose Evolution

Purpose is alive.

It can evolve.

Every update creates a new version.

Previous versions remain stored.

---

# Success Metric

The engine succeeds when the user gains clarity.

Not when a form is completed.
