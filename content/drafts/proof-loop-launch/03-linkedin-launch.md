Aura is what people think you are. Proof is what you've actually done.

Today we're shipping Proof Loop inside BELONG — a structured way to turn a claim, a commitment, or a goal into something that can actually face evidence and real-world action.

Here's the shape of it:

PROVE IT — publish a claim, prediction, capability, or measurable goal, with success criteria declared up front.

SHOW UP — the Proof converts into a real BELONG Project, with concrete roles other people can join. Contribution isn't limited to money — time, skill, expertise, and logistics all count.

BUILD — participants execute together and log an evidence timeline as work happens, not after the fact.

IMPACT — when the criteria or deadline are met, the Proof resolves to an Outcome (supported, partial, mixed, not supported, or inconclusive — all legitimate) and generates a shareable Impact Receipt.

BELONG — the resolved Proof lives on the participant's profile as a durable record, not a vanity metric.

We built this because most networks organize people around content. We think there's more value in organizing around proof, participation, and action — testable claims instead of hot takes, and a record of what got built instead of how many people liked it.

This is live in BELONG now. Open a Proof to see the full loop, or start your own.
