# BELONG — Product Vision

**Status:** Frozen · July 2026  
**Audience:** Product, Engineering, Design, Leadership

---

## Mission

BELONG is the platform where people build meaningful lives, communities, and real impact — together.

We exist to turn intent into momentum: connecting builders, communities, projects, and events around what each person is actually trying to create.

---

## Problem

Modern productivity and social tools optimize for output or attention — not belonging. People with ambitious goals (startups, careers, health, community, creative work) lack:

- A **single home** for what they are building
- **Purpose-aligned communities** (not generic feeds)
- **Shared projects and events** tied to real missions
- **Trust and continuity** across relationships

---

## Product Promise

> Build a life that matters — with people who get it.

BELONG gives every member:

1. **A mission** — what they are building and why
2. **A network** — connections that compound
3. **A workspace** — projects, events, messages in one shell
4. **Guidance** — contextual next steps (AI-assisted over time)

---

## Target Users

| Segment | Build goal | Core need |
|---------|------------|-----------|
| Founders | Startup | Co-builders, community, project hub |
| Professionals | Career | Mentors, events, accountability |
| Learners | Learn | Communities, structured progress |
| Creators | Creator | Audience, collaboration, launches |
| Community leaders | Community | Members, events, shared missions |
| General builders | Health, Relationships, Travel | Support network + clarity |

Onboarding captures **build goal** and **vision** — every downstream experience personalizes from this.

---

## Product Pillars

### 1. Mission-first identity
Profile, dashboard, and discovery center on *what you are building*, not vanity metrics.

### 2. Community as infrastructure
Communities are first-class — not a sidebar feature. Discovery, membership, and events flow through them.

### 3. Build in public (with control)
Projects and events make work visible to the right people. Progress and participation are real data.

### 4. Calm, premium UX
Apple × Linear × Notion quality: dark, focused, fast. One design language across every screen.

### 5. Intelligence that helps
AI surfaces **actionable next steps** from real activity — never fake content or placeholder feeds.

---

## Core User Journeys

```
Register/OAuth → Onboarding (build goal) → Home Dashboard
       ↓
Explore Community → Join → Participate in Events
       ↓
Create/Join Projects → Message collaborators
       ↓
Refine Mission & Profile → Grow network (Connections)
```

## Success Metrics (North Star framework)

| Metric | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| **Activated builders** | Completed onboarding + 1 community or project within 7 days |
| **Weekly builders** | WAU with ≥1 project/event/message action |
| **Mission clarity** | % profiles with primary mission + build goal |
| **Network density** | Accepted connections per active user |
| **Retention** | D30 return rate |

---

## Non-Goals (v1)

- Public social feed / infinite scroll
- Crypto, tokens, or engagement bait
- Generic CRM or enterprise sales tooling
- Unmoderated open posting at scale

---

## Long-Term Vision

BELONG becomes the **operating system for intentional life-building**:

- AI copilot for mission, matching, and project planning
- Marketplace for services between builders (future)
- Global events and regional communities
- APIs and mobile clients on the same engine architecture
